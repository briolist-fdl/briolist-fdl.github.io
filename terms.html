Terms of use

Updated 25 September 2026

Purpose and availability

BrioBots Backups is a private administrative tool maintained by briolist-fdl for backing up databases used by BrioBots. It is in development and is not a public hosted backup service. This website does not offer subscriptions, payments or public account registration.

Authorized use

Only connect Google accounts and databases that you are authorized to administer. Use the tool in accordance with applicable service rules and respect the privacy of people whose information appears in a database.

Operator responsibilities

The operator selects the source database and backup destination, protects credentials and decryption keys, monitors failures, manages retention and tests recovery. A successful upload alone does not establish that an archive can be restored. Losing the decryption key may make encrypted archives unusable.

Service limitations

No uninterrupted availability, recovery time or successful restoration is promised. Google Drive, the database provider and any runner infrastructure are separate services with their own terms and availability. These terms do not limit rights that cannot lawfully be excluded.

Stopping use

The operator may stop the job and revoke Google access at any time. Stopping access does not automatically remove existing backups. See the privacy notice for data handling and deletion information.

Contact

Contact briolist-fdl on GitHub with questions about this tool.
