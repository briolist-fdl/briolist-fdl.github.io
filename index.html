PRIVATE OPERATOR TOOL

BrioBots Backups

BrioBots Backups protects databases used by these tools. It encrypts database exports before uploading them to the operator’s Google Drive and verifies the uploaded files. It is not offered as a public backup service.

Google sign-in connects the operator’s account to the backup tool. Discord members do not need to connect a Google account. The application requests access to files it creates or files explicitly made available to it, not general access to all files in Google Drive.
